## How I Approach Analytics

1. **Define the decision:** Clarify the problem, stakeholders, success measures, and constraints.
2. **Establish trusted data:** Confirm data grain, reconcile sources, and validate metric definitions.
3. **Diagnose and model:** Explore drivers, test assumptions, and compare scenarios.
4. **Communicate the implications:** Explain findings, uncertainty, and business trade-offs.
5. **Operationalise and monitor:** Automate repeatable work and track outcomes against expectations.

I prioritise solutions that are **reliable, explainable, reusable, and useful to the people making decisions**.
